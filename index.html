ABOUT AANDPUR

A better way to learn.
A clearer path to progress.

Aandpur is built with a simple idea — students should have access to structured, reliable, and effective learning resources in one place.

We are building Aandpur as a student-first learning platform where studying becomes more organized, interactive, and effective.

From chapter-wise notes and previous year questions to practice problems and quizzes, resources are designed to support students throughout their learning journey.
